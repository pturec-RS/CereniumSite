Common Questions

What health systems typically ask us.

Does Cerenium work with health systems on Oracle Health Cloud (OCI)?

Yes. Cerenium Technologies supports Oracle Health Cloud environments as well as on-premises and hybrid Cerner Millennium deployments. We help health systems assess, plan, and execute cloud migration strategies on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure.

What size health systems does Cerenium typically serve?

Our sweet spot is midmarket health systems — roughly 50 to 500 beds — including community hospitals, regional health systems, critical access hospitals, and multi-facility specialty networks. These organizations benefit most from our specialized, senior-led approach.

Can Cerenium support an ongoing Oracle Health environment rather than a one-time project?

Absolutely. Many of our client relationships are ongoing managed services or retainer-based engagements. We believe EHR value is created continuously — not just at go-live — and we structure our services accordingly.

How quickly can Cerenium mobilize for an urgent Oracle Health need?

For time-sensitive issues — go-live stabilization, critical system performance problems, or urgent optimization needs — we can typically mobilize a senior consultant within 48–72 hours. Contact us directly to discuss urgent engagements.
